## How components shared between members are credited

Several parts of the final product were built in stages by more than one member. The map credits them as follows, using the members' own files:

| Component | Credit |
|---|---|
| Loan calculation | Loan amount, fees and emergency fund: Phương Anh. Loan term by age and the 1% prepayment fee: Sơn. |
| Result screen | First dashboard framework on mock data: Tuấn. Version-5 content (Step 4 table, source links, conclusion content, cash-flow table, summary box): Phương Anh. Final deep-gradient design (3 tabs, sliders, progress bars, SVG chart, comparison-point and today's-money buttons): Sơn, with An on layout. |
| Filters | Filter logic and "Same filters as Buy": Nam Phương. Their place in the final interface: Sơn. |
| Workbook corrections | Series collected by An and Nam Phương. Income, unit consistency and formula recalculation: An. Gold and VN-Index: Nam Phương. Code-to-workbook comparison (48 parameters): Phương Anh. |
